# 2026 CISC7021 Applied NLP

## Course Project

### Section outline

In this group project (form your group here), you will work with a team on a specific research topic or task related to NLP/LLMs. You are encouraged to initiate new ideas and methods to address the weaknesses of current work. The objective of this project is to provide you with an opportunity to identify a research problem and gain experience in working with practical NLP/LLM systems. A list of survey papers related to LLMs is provided in the section: "Survey Papers in LLMs."

### Project Requirement

The project will be conducted in two phases: paper reading and project development.

- **Paper Reading**: In the first phase, you are to select one or more papers relevant to your topic of interest. You will read these papers and compile a review report detailing your insights and understanding of the topic.
- **Project Development**: The second phase involves system implementation. You will present your work at the end of the semester and submit a final report formatted according to ACL meeting standards. The project may involve experimental work where you replicate and evaluate the model proposed in the selected research paper, or you may propose advancements to current methods of study.

### Phrase 1 (Paper Reading)

#### Task 1: Review (Due Date: 10th Oct)

You are required to submit a Review Report that should include:

- A list of papers you have read, including their references, to better understand the basics.
- The main methodologies previously used in related topics.
- The experimental results obtained from the works you reviewed.
- Your project idea and preliminary strategy (methodology).
- A tentative schedule for the completion of your project tasks.

### Phrase 2 (System Implementation)

#### Task 2: Final Report (Due date: 17th Nov)

You are required to submit a Final Report following the two-column format of ACL proceedings. The final report should include at least the following sections:

- **Introduction**: Provide a concise summary of your project's motivation and achievements. Include a brief literature review of the papers you have read and summarize the conclusions drawn.
- **Detailed Description**: Discuss your work in detail, including design, implementation, testing, and evaluation. Use diagrams, flowcharts, and accompanying explanations wherever possible to better describe the problems addressed.
- **Conclusion**: Share the experiences you have learned, the problems and difficulties encountered, and suggest future improvements, if any.

#### Task 3: Pre-record Presentation (Due Date: 17th Nov)

You are required to pre-record a Presentation and submit the following items in UMMoodle:

- **Slides File**: Each group must pre-record a 5-minute presentation to introduce their work, with all members participating.
- **Source Code**: Optionally, submit all source code in a zip file. Include comments in your source code wherever appropriate.
- **Final Results**: Provide the results obtained after running your program.
- **Presentation Video**: Upload the presentation video to any public website (e.g., YouTube) and complete the shared form with your information.

#### Task 4: In-class Presentation (17th Nov and 24th Nov)

During the final class, a presentation and Q&A session will be organized, giving students the opportunity to showcase their work. Due to time constraints, the presentation schedule will be as follows:

- **Presentation Time**: Each group will have 4 mins for their presentation and 1 min Q&A, which must be given by a designated representative.
- **Mandatory Attendance**: All students are required to attend this presentation session and evaluate the presenting group. Attendance will be taken, and students who do not attend will receive a deduction in their grade.
- **Powerpoint**: To smooth the arrangement of presentation during the class, we will provide a laptop. You need to submit your presentation Powerpoint here by 14:00 17th Nov.
