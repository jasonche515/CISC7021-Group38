1. Why did LLMLingua lose to simple truncation?
"We have hypotheses, but we did not test them directly. LLMLingua removes words one by one, so sentences become broken. A 3B model has a hard time understanding broken text. Truncation keeps whole passages, so what it keeps is clean. Also, most answers in this dataset are near the start, which helps truncation."

(中文提示:這是假設,不是已證明的原因。)

2. Part 4 was meant to beat truncation. Why did it fail?
"Part 4 fixed the chunking problem and made the compressor question-aware. F1 went up by 0.044 and 0.059, but it is still about 0.08 to 0.10 below truncation. We changed five things at once, so we cannot say which one helped. Also, the compressor was slow, 15 to 17 seconds per question, which hurts speed."

3. How do you know the loss at 4x comes from the answer being cut?
"We located each gold answer in the original document. When the answer is kept, F1 is about 0.47 to 0.49. When it is cut, F1 is about 0.20. The share of cut answers goes from 21% to 37% at 4x. But this is only a pattern. Questions with early answers might also be easier, and 87 of 150 answer locations are approximate."

4. F1 is only 0.43 without compression. Is the model just weak?
"F1 measures word overlap, not meaning. Gold answers are often full sentences written by people, and the model may answer correctly with different words. A 3B model also limits the score. Other models on this task get similar numbers, for example 36.8 for Llama2-7B-chat in the LongBench paper. So we focus on the change relative to the baseline, not the absolute value."

(這題引用的 36.8 來自我先前查到的 LongBench 結果,請在報告中確認出處後再用。)

5. Is the latency result reliable? Why do the numbers differ between tables?
"Our four parts ran on four different machines, so we only compare latency from one rerun on a single RTX 3060: 9.4 seconds uncompressed, 5.3 for 2x, 3.2 for 4x. The main table shows per-part latencies on different machines, so they should not be compared with each other. Part 4 has no same-machine measurement, so we do not make a speed ratio for it."

Is the 2x loss real? "In our main run, no. The confidence interval includes zero. In the same-machine rerun it was marginal. We call it a small loss at the edge of significance."

Why only one dataset and one model? "Time and compute limits. We say clearly that the ranking is shown only for this setup."
